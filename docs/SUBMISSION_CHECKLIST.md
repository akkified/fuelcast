# Submission Checklist: 2026 GATSA App Development Pitch

**Deadline:** upload to **myGATSA** by **9:00 PM, Friday, September 25, 2026**. The rally (and awards) is October 7 at Six Flags Over Georgia.
**What you submit:** *only* the pitch video, as an **.mp4**, **3 minutes or less**.

## Eligibility rules

- [ ] One entry per chapter (an individual or a team of up to 6). Confirm with your advisor that no one else in your chapter is entering.
- [ ] The entry is your chapter's own work, specific to your chapter. Put your chapter name in the video. Ask your advisor about the policy on AI-assisted development.
- [ ] Created during this school year. The git history starts in September 2026.

## The video must include

- [ ] **The theme**: say "Fitness and Nutrition" out loud (script, 0:40)
- [ ] **A problem statement** (script, 0:12–0:40)
- [ ] **A target audience**: high-school athletes, ages 14–18 (script, 0:12–0:40)
- [ ] **An app demonstration and walkthrough** showing **at least 4 key screens**. The script shows 9.
- [ ] **A technical explanation** of how the app works (script, 2:10–2:45)
- [ ] **Length of 3:00 or less.** Check the exported file's duration.

## Before you record

- [ ] `npm install` then `npx expo start`; scan the QR code with Expo Go on the iPhone
- [ ] Load the sample athlete (Settings → Load sample athlete, or "Explore with a sample athlete")
- [ ] Record between 2:45 and 3:15 PM on a **Tuesday** so "Right now: Top-off snack" and "Game tomorrow: keep it light" both appear
- [ ] **Form Check demo:** film a teammate doing 2–3 squats **from the side** (whole body in frame, good light), and one "bad" rep (a quarter squat) so the video shows a fix. Run Form Check once on Wi-Fi beforehand so the pose model is downloaded.
- [ ] Decide on the AI Coach demo: a live Claude key (ask a parent or guardian), or offline mode (use the quick prompts and adjust one narration line)
- [ ] Do Not Disturb on, full battery, max brightness
- [ ] Run `npm test` and confirm it still says 89 passing (the script quotes that number)
- [ ] Read the Insights number off the screen for the `[READ THE NUMBER ON SCREEN]` line
- [ ] Check the NFHS participation figure (docs/RESEARCH.md, reference 1)

## Export and upload

- [ ] 1080p, H.264, `.mp4`
- [ ] Captions on
- [ ] Watch the whole thing once with sound; check that no notifications or personal info are visible
- [ ] Upload to myGATSA and confirm the upload finished before 9:00 PM Friday

## Optional (not scored, but good to have)

- [ ] Push the code to GitHub (see README) so you can show judges the repo if asked
- [ ] Keep the app on your phone for the rally on October 7, in case anyone wants a live demo
